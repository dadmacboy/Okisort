Apartment trash guide

Rules follow Global Vision Housing guidance. Unlisted chemicals, construction materials, and unusually large items should be confirmed with Global Vision.
🔥 Burnable
  • Raw garbage and diapers
  • Shampoo and detergent containers
  • Plastic grocery bags and food containers
  • CDs, DVDs, leather and vinyl goods
  • Aluminum foil
♻️ Recyclable
  • Glass bottles
  • Cans
  • PET beverage bottles
  • Paper and cardboard
⚫ Non-combustible
  • Glassware, plates and mugs
  • Knives and razors
  • Pans, kettles and frying pans
  • Umbrellas, wire hangers and small appliances
  • LED and incandescent light bulbs
⚠️ Hazardous / Special Handling
  • Fluorescent light bulbs
  • Mercury thermometers
  • Batteries
  • Lighters
🛋️ Bulk Items
  • Anything that does not fit in a 45-liter trash bag
  • Furniture, appliances, blankets and pillows
  • Contact the collection service before disposal
