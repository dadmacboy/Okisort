Sorting guide

🔥 Burnable

Kitchen waste, clothing, many household plastics, leather, rubber, and non-recyclable paper. Drain liquids and remove batteries or large metal parts when practical.

♻️ Recyclables

Separate ordinary glass bottles, cans, eligible PET bottles, clean waste paper, and eligible residential yard waste. Rinse and prepare each stream as directed.

⚫ Non-burnable

Glassware, ceramics, cutlery, cookware, sharp metal, and small appliances. Wrap blades, broken glass, and sharp pieces securely.

⚠️ Hazardous

Fluorescent lamps, mercury instruments, empty lighters, and eligible household dry-cell batteries. Protect lamps from breakage and remove lighter fuel completely.

🛋️ Bulk

Items that cannot fit in the designated bag generally require advance application and a disposal ticket.

🚫 Not collected normally

Regulated home appliances, personal computers, rechargeable/button batteries, automotive waste, chemicals, medical sharps, soil, and other excluded items require a retailer, manufacturer, specialist, medical provider, or authority.
